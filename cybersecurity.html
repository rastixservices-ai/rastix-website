The RASTIX approach

Security-first, not security-bolted-on

Most security breaches aren't caused by sophisticated zero-day exploits. They're caused by misconfigurations, unpatched systems, weak credentials, and gaps in monitoring. RASTIX builds security into everything we do, rather than adding it as an afterthought.

We align to industry frameworks including the Essential Eight, ISO 27001, and NIST CSF, giving you a defensible, measurable security posture that satisfies clients and regulators alike.

Essential Eight ISO 27001 NIST CSF Microsoft Sentinel CrowdStrike Defender for Endpoint Zero Trust SIEM / SOAR

  • Vulnerability management

    Continuous scanning, prioritisation, and remediation of vulnerabilities across your entire environment.

  • Identity & access management

    Zero Trust principles, MFA enforcement, and privileged access management to control who can access what.

  • Threat detection & response

    24/7 monitoring with SIEM/SOAR tooling to detect, alert on, and respond to threats before they escalate.

  • Security awareness

    Staff training and phishing simulations to reduce human risk, the most common attack vector in modern breaches.
